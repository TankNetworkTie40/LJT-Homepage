Junteng Liu contributed to this work as a co-author. C-Eval is a meticulously curated multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite that assesses advanced knowledge and reasoning capabilities across foundation models, covering 52 different disciplines and 4 difficulty levels from elementary to advanced professional domains.
