Junteng Liu is the first author of this work. This work systematically investigates the understudied chart understanding capability of modern vision-language models, identifies critical perception bottleneck issues that severely limit their performance on understanding structured chart information, and proposes targeted mitigation strategies, with open-source Vision4Chart code repository publicly available.
