Junteng Liu contributed to this work as a co-author. This work addresses the critical hallucination problem in large language models from a novel inner representation perspective, proposing the in-context sharpness metric to detect factually incorrect generations before output, achieving significant hallucination mitigation performance without additional fine-tuning.
